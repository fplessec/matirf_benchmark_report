\section{Adam --- general MAP minimiser}
\label{sec:adam}

\paragraph{How it works.}
Adam minimises the objective~\eqref{eq:objective} by gradient descent with a per-coordinate
adaptive step, positivity by clamping, a learning rate halved on any loss increase, and a
small-change stop:
\begin{quote}
$f \leftarrow f_0$ \hfill\textit{(start: the ridge $s_2^2$ estimate)}\\
\textbf{repeat} for each iteration:\\
\hspace{1.2em}$g \leftarrow \nabla L(f)$ \hfill\textit{(gradient of the energy)}\\
\hspace{1.2em}$f \leftarrow f - \mathrm{lr}\cdot \hat m/(\sqrt{\hat v}+\varepsilon)$ \hfill\textit{(Adam step)}\\
\hspace{1.2em}$f \leftarrow \max(f,0)$ \hfill\textit{(project onto $f\ge0$)}\\
\hspace{1.2em}\textbf{every} $K$ steps: halve $\mathrm{lr}$ if the loss rose; stop if $|\Delta L|<\text{EPS}$
\end{quote}
\noindent where $\hat m,\hat v$ are Adam's running estimates of the gradient and its square,
$\mathrm{lr}$ the learning rate, $\varepsilon$ a tiny stabiliser, $K$ the check interval, EPS the
plateau threshold. $L$ is convex, so with a prior that weighs the minimiser is unique and Adam
reaches it --- the reconstruction is then only as good as the analytic prior $R$.

\paragraph{Key parameters.}
Only the prior \texttt{reg} and its share $\lambda$ move the solution. We compare \textbf{L1}
(sparsity) and \textbf{Tikhonov} (the $\ell_2$ of the gradient, smoothness); Tikhonov is degree-2,
so at the small scale of $f$ it is ${\sim}50\times$ weaker for a given $\lambda$ and over-smooths.
The rest are fixed convergence knobs: $\mathrm{lr}=0.1$, the ridge $s_2^2$ start, and a large budget
with $\text{EPS}=10^{-8}$ so the plateau --- not the budget --- ends the run.

\paragraph{Limit.}
Adam has no failure of its own --- it minimises the energy exactly --- so on MA-TIRF the ceiling
is entirely the analytic prior's, and none models a cell's depth structure as well as a learned
denoiser. The only failures are at the ends of $\lambda$: $\lambda\to0$ is noisy least squares,
$\lambda\to1$ drops the data. Adam is the right \emph{engine} and, on MA-TIRF, the wrong
\emph{prior}.
