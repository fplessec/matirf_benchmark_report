\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

The campaign is a theory-guided \emph{atlas}: for each solver we sweep only the axes the analysis
says move the \emph{solution}, and fix the convergence knobs. The physical operator is used
throughout ($\texttt{normalize}=$\texttt{False}) and $g$ is peak-normalised. Runs are isolated,
timed and resumable (\texttt{benchmarks/runner.py}). This report uses the minimal pass (one
composite truth, tightened grids); the full atlas (two truths, wider grids) is in
\texttt{benchmarks/campaign.py} for a deeper run.

\subsection{Datasets and noise}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllc}
\toprule
problem & dataset & kind & noise levels \\
\midrule
MA-TIRF & \texttt{cell\_fibres\_vesicles} & synthetic (has truth), composite & $0,\ 0.02,\ 0.05$ \\
MA-TIRF & \texttt{esoubies} & real (no truth) & native \\
deconv  & \texttt{img\_001} & synthetic (has truth) & $0,\ 0.02,\ 0.05$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Noise is added Gaussian ($\sigma$ relative to the peak); with added noise the oracle variance is
used, else the noise is estimated from $g$.

\subsection{Parameter grids (the atlas axes)}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
solver & swept grid (minimal pass) \\
\midrule
ADAM     & $R\in\{$none, TV, SHV$\}\times\lambda\in\{.05,.1,.2,.35\}$ \\
ADMM     & $\kappa\in\{.01,.1,.3\}\times\mu\in\{.5,1\}$ \\
PnP      & denoiser$\in\{$Gaussian, Bilateral$\}\times\sigma\in\{25,50\}$, $\lambda_{kz}=10^{-2}$ \\
ADMM-PnP & denoiser$\in\{$Gaussian, Bilateral$\}\times\sigma\in\{25,50\}\times\rho\in\{.1,1\}$ \\
MCMC     & denoiser $=$ TV Bregman $\times\ \sigma\in\{.01,.02,.03\}$ \\
PPXA     & confirmation only (TV/SHV, $\lambda=.1$): Adam $=$ PPXA \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Each solver also runs its no-prior / no-denoiser baseline. Grid endpoints are chosen to span the
predicted useful bands \emph{and} the failure edges (e.g.\ ADAM $\lambda=.35$, ADMM $\kappa=.3$)
so the limits of Sec.~\ref{sec:limits} are visible.

\subsection{Iterations, stopping and initialisation}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllll}
\toprule
solver & iterations & stop & step & init \\
\midrule
ADAM     & $\le 3000$ & $\text{EPS}=10^{-6}$ (every $K{=}10$) & $\text{lr}=0.1$ & ridge, $\lambda_{rr}=s_2^2$ \\
PPXA     & $\le 3000$ & $\text{EPS}=10^{-6}$ & $\gamma=0.01$, relax $1.5$ & ridge, $s_2^2$ \\
ADMM     & $200$ fixed & --- & $\mu$ (dual step) & data step, ridge $\mu$ \\
PnP      & $16$ fixed & --- (annealing) & schedule $\alpha_k$ & ridge, $s_2^2$ \\
ADMM-PnP & $50$ fixed & --- & $\rho$ & ridge, $s_2^2$ \\
MCMC     & $300$ ($25\%$ burn-in) & --- & $\sigma$, temp.\ (auto) & ridge, $s_2^2$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Only Adam and PPXA stop early (the loss plateaus on the near-flat MA-TIRF valley; a tight EPS
would chase negligible improvements, so it is set to $10^{-6}$). The others run a fixed budget
the notes show is enough --- except PnP, whose $16$ steps are a short annealing schedule, not a
convergence. No run starts from the adjoint (${\sim}850\times$ too large on MA-TIRF).

\subsection{Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}
Each metric answers one question; truth-comparison metrics come from \texttt{core.metrics}
(scale-aligned, since $f$ is fixed only up to a positive factor), \texttt{chi2\_ratio} from the
runner (it needs no truth).
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
metric & question & where \\
\midrule
NMSE            & overall error ($0$ perfect) & MA-TIRF, deconv \\
PSNR (dB)       & same, in the image-processing currency (companion) & MA-TIRF, deconv \\
Depth Error (nm)& \textbf{axial localisation} --- the MA-TIRF question & MA-TIRF \\
Stack Recovery  & axial super-resolution ($\ge 30$~nm pairs resolved) & MA-TIRF \\
SSIM            & structural similarity (reliable in 2D) & deconv only \\
chi2\_ratio     & data fit without a truth ($\approx 1$ good) & all, incl.\ \texttt{esoubies} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
PSNR is monotone in NMSE (same ranking) --- kept as the familiar dB companion, not an
independent axis. SSIM is unreliable on the anisotropic MA-TIRF volume, so it is deconv-only.
Each run also records runtime, peak memory and status (ok / rejected / OOM / timeout): a solver
that OOMs on a real stack is disqualified by that alone, whatever its NMSE.
