\section{The algorithms}
\label{sec:algorithms}

All six consume the same \texttt{core.Objective} and forward operator, so there is one
implementation of each, shared by both problems. Full derivations and per-parameter analysis are
in \texttt{matirf\_python\_plus/docs/algorithms/}; this section is a one-paragraph recall.

\paragraph{Adam / PPXA --- general MAP minimisers.}
Both minimise Eq.~\eqref{eq:objective} to the same optimum (Adam by gradient descent, PPXA by
parallel proximal steps), so the reconstruction is only as good as the analytic prior $R$. Key
parameter: $\lambda$ (the regularisation share) with a chosen $R$ (TV, SHV, \dots). Adam's step
$\text{lr}$ and PPXA's $\gamma$ set speed, not the solution.

\paragraph{ADMM --- sparsity (soft-threshold) prior.}
Alternating data step, soft-threshold, dual update. The prior is $\ell_1$ sparsity, applied by
the threshold $\kappa$; $\mu$ is the penalty (and, coupled, the dual step, capped near $1.618$).
Excellent on sparse objects, ill-suited to diffuse ones.

\paragraph{PnP-HQS --- plug-and-play denoiser, annealing schedule.}
Half-quadratic splitting where a denoiser \emph{is} the prior, run over a short annealing
schedule (not to convergence). Key: the denoiser, $\sigma$ (denoising level), $\lambda_{kz}$
(final data weight). Scale-free (it feeds the denoiser $255\,f/\text{peak}$).

\paragraph{ADMM-PnP --- plug-and-play denoiser, dual variable.}
The augmented-Lagrangian counterpart of PnP: same denoiser prior, but a dual variable instead of
a schedule, run to a fixed point. Key: the denoiser, $\sigma$, and $\rho$ (penalty); the prior
strength scales as $\rho\sigma^2$.

\paragraph{MCMC --- MMSE by posterior sampling.}
A Metropolis-Hastings chain on the data term $D$, with a denoiser as the prior; it returns the
mean of the states after burn-in. Key: the denoiser (TV Bregman here), $\sigma$ (exploration
and denoising, a fraction of the peak), and the acceptance temperature (calibrated).
