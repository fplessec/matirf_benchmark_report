\section{Intrinsic limits}
\label{sec:limits}

These are the walls no parameter choice moves. On MA-TIRF they are all the same wall seen from a
different side --- the ${\sim}47$-direction null space (Sec.~\ref{sec:problem}) --- and the only
lever against it is the prior. Full analysis with provenance in
\texttt{docs/algorithms/limits.md}.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{2.1cm}p{6.2cm}p{4.6cm}}
\toprule
algorithm & intrinsic wall & can it be moved? \\
\midrule
Adam / PPXA & only as good as the analytic prior $R$; $\lambda\!\to\!0$ noisy/collapsed,
             $\lambda\!\to\!1$ empty/flat & not by the optimiser --- needs a better prior \\
ADMM        & $\ell_1$ is the wrong prior for diffuse cells; $\kappa$ high empties the image;
             $\mu>1.618$ diverges & no --- the wall is the sparsity prior \\
PnP-HQS     & denoiser scale mismatch + collapse under noise; short schedule & scale fixed by
             $\div$peak; ceiling is the denoiser \\
ADMM-PnP    & same denoiser ceiling; $\rho\sigma^2$ coupling; OOM/timeout on full real volumes
            & needs tiling + a better 3D denoiser \\
MCMC        & the Metropolis test on $D$ is \textbf{blind to the null space} on MA-TIRF & no on
             MA-TIRF (structural); works on deconvolution \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Three problem-level limits sit under all of them on MA-TIRF: the huge null space (depth
collapse), noise amplified out of proportion along the weak directions, and photograph-scale
defaults being ${\sim}40\times$ wrong. MCMC's is the sharpest result: its exploration lives in
$D$'s null space, which by definition does not change $D$, so the acceptance test cannot see it
--- no setting beats the ridge start on MA-TIRF, while on well-posed deconvolution it works.
