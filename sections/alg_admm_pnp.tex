\section{ADMM-PnP --- a denoiser as the prior, with a dual variable}
\label{sec:admm_pnp}

\paragraph{How it works.}
ADMM-PnP is the augmented-Lagrangian counterpart of PnP: the same split, but a \textbf{dual
variable} $u$ instead of a schedule, so $\rho,\sigma$ stay fixed and it runs to a fixed point:
\begin{quote}
$x \leftarrow f_0$;\quad $v \leftarrow x$;\quad $u \leftarrow 0$ \hfill\textit{(start; $u$ is the dual variable)}\\
\textbf{repeat} for each iteration:\\
\hspace{1.2em}$x \leftarrow (H^{\!\top}\!H+\rho I)^{-1}(H^{\!\top} g + \rho(v-u))$ \hfill\textit{(data step: fit $g$, pulled to $v-u$)}\\
\hspace{1.2em}$v \leftarrow \max\!\big(\tfrac{\text{peak}}{255}D_\sigma(255(x+u)/\text{peak}),\,0\big)$ \hfill\textit{(denoise at fixed $\sigma$, then $v\ge0$)}\\
\hspace{1.2em}$u \leftarrow u + (x-v)$ \hfill\textit{(dual update)}
\end{quote}
\noindent where $D_\sigma$ is the denoiser at fixed level $\sigma$, $\rho$ the penalty, $x$ the data
copy, $v$ the denoised copy, $u$ the dual variable that accumulates the mismatch. The dual, not a
schedule, fills the null space.

\paragraph{Key parameters.}
The denoiser, $\sigma$, and $\rho$. The prior's strength is the product $\rho\sigma^2$ (confirmed:
at fixed $\rho\sigma^2$ the result is unchanged; the sweet spot is $\rho\sigma^2\approx70$).
Crucially $\rho$ is \emph{not} the eigenvalue threshold that $\lambda_{kz}$ is in PnP --- the dual
does the null-space filling, so a \emph{low} $\rho$ (moderate prior) wins and a large $\rho$
over-smooths. The opposite of PnP.

\paragraph{Results.}
Typically the strongest reconstructor under noise. The best denoiser depends on the object
(Table~\ref{tab:denoisers}), with one firm rule: \textbf{TV~Bregman collapses in the dual
iteration} (its data fit blows up) on every structure --- Gaussian wins on the membrane, DCT on the
sparse structures under noise.

\paragraph{Limit.}
The family's limits: the denoiser must be fed at the right scale ($\div\text{peak}$), and a 3D
denoise on the full volume every iteration is the memory bind --- ADMM-PnP peaked near
\SI{10}{\giga\byte} and ran out of memory on the real \texttt{esoubies} stack. It needs a
tiling/precision budget before running on full real volumes.
